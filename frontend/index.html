Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about FinFamily.

What is FinFamily?

FinFamily is a web application that brings a family's assets, loans, income, expenses and insurance into a single dashboard so you can see your real net worth and plan how to clear debt.

Can several family members use the same account?

Yes. You create a family workspace, invite members or let them request to join, and an admin approves access. Every change is recorded in an audit trail.

Does FinFamily support Indian loans such as gold loans and EMIs?

Yes. The loan engine calculates EMIs and repayment schedules, supports part-payments and rate revisions, and includes dedicated gold loan tracking. Amounts are shown in Indian rupees.

How do I get started?

Sign up with a username and password, create or join a family, then add your assets, income and loans.
