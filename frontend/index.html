    I have too many loans. Which one should I close first?

    FinFamily compares Debt Avalanche (highest interest first), Debt Snowball (smallest balance first) and a custom-weighted plan, for a lump sum or a monthly surplus. It shows the months and interest each plan saves, so you can see which loan to close first.

    How does the AI advisor help me close loans faster?

    On the Debt Strategies page you can ask the AI advisor, powered by Google Gemini, for a personalised suggestion on how to split your extra money across your loans. It's optional and works from the numbers you entered.

    Do I need real loan account numbers or bank details?

    No. FinFamily is for your own reference. Add a loan with just the type, amount, interest rate, tenure and start date. The lender is optional and only a label — there is no account-number field, no bank login and no statement upload.

    Does FinFamily connect to my bank?

    No. You enter the numbers yourself, so it works with any bank, lender or informal loan, and nothing is shared with a bank.

    What is FinFamily?

    FinFamily is a web application that brings a family's assets, loans, income, expenses and insurance into a single dashboard so you can see your real net worth and plan how to clear debt.

    Can I track house construction, a business or rental income?

    Yes. Expenses has a Household tab for daily spending and a Projects tab for things like a house build or a new business. Link loans and rental income to a project to see how it was funded, the rental yield and the payback period.

    Can several family members use the same account?

    Yes. You create a family workspace, invite members or let them request to join, and an admin approves access. Every change is recorded in an audit trail.

    Does FinFamily support Indian loans such as gold loans and EMIs?

    Yes. The loan engine calculates EMIs and repayment schedules, supports part-payments and rate revisions, and includes dedicated gold loan tracking. Amounts are shown in Indian rupees.

    What is FairShare?

    FairShare is the second app in the same login. It splits trip and event expenses between households and shows exactly who pays whom.

    Is FinFamily free?

    Yes. Creating an account and using FinFamily is free.
